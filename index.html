Exam Instructions

Select an SOP from “My SOP Allocations” to begin.

Before you begin

  • The exam has 5 questions drawn randomly from the SOP's question bank.
  • Time limit: 10 minutes. The exam auto-submits when time runs out.
  • Passing score: 80% (4 of 5 correct).
  • The exam is closed-book — do not refer to the SOP during the attempt.
  • A failed attempt is re-allocated for retraining; you may re-attempt after re-reading the SOP.
